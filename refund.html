Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Thank you for purchasing from Veylivo. This policy explains our approach to cancellations, refunds, and issues relating to our digital products.

1. Digital Products

Veylivo offers digital products that are delivered electronically. Because digital products can be accessed or downloaded after purchase, purchases are generally non-refundable once the product has been delivered or access has been provided.

2. Before Purchasing

Please review the product description, included materials, price, and other available information before completing your purchase. If you have a question about a product, please contact us before purchasing.

3. Refund Requests

If you experience a technical problem, receive an incorrect product, or believe there is another genuine issue with your order, contact Veylivo support as soon as possible. We will review the issue and, where appropriate, may provide a replacement, correction, or refund.

4. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, please contact us with the relevant order details. Verified duplicate payments will be reviewed for an appropriate resolution.

5. Cancellation

A cancellation request may be considered only before digital access or delivery has been provided. Once the digital product has been delivered or access has been provided, cancellation may not be available.

6. How to Contact Us

For refund, cancellation, or order-related questions, please contact Veylivo using the contact information provided on our Contact page.

Important: Any refund approved under this policy will be processed through the applicable payment method or payment platform, subject to its processing procedures.

7. Policy Changes

Veylivo may update this policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this website with the updated date shown above.
